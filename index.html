About Big Billy

Where architecture
meets desire

Born in Ghana and refined at Parsons School of Design in New York, Big Billy established his atelier on the belief that fashion should move like a second skin an intimate dialogue between fabric and the body it embraces.

His work is defined by a commitment to material integrity, sourcing premium silks and technical textiles from long-standing heritage ateliers. By combining traditional craftsmanship with modern silhouettes, he has earned recognition in emerging designer showcases for his focus on longevity and construction. Every collection is a deliberate study in the "memory of hands" ensuring the human element remains at the core of the digital age.
