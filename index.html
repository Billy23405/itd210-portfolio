About Big Billy

Where architecture
meets desire

Born in Ghana and refined at Parsons School of Design in New York, Big Billy established his atelier on the belief that fashion should move like a second skin an intimate dialogue between fabric and the body it embraces.

His collections have illuminated the runways of Milan Fashion Week for over a decade, earning retrospectives at the Museo della Moda and permanent placements at the Victoria and Albert Museum. He collaborates closely with the silk ateliers of Como and master dyers in Kyoto, ensuring every piece carries the memory of hands.
